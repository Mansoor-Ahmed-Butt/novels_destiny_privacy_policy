Overview & Platform Scope

Welcome to Novels Destiny ("we", "our", or "us"). Novels Destiny is a state-of-the-art mobile application designed to connect readers with captivating digital literature and empower authors to write, format, dictate, and publish rich multi-chapter stories and PDF manuscripts.

This Privacy Policy governs your access to and use of the Novels Destiny application across supported mobile devices (including Android smartphones and tablets) and associated backend infrastructure. By downloading, accessing, or creating an account within Novels Destiny, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and consented to the practices described in this document.

If you do not agree with this policy in whole or in part, you should discontinue using the app immediately and may request the permanent removal of your account and associated records as detailed in Section 10.

03

Microphone & Audio Policy (Sensitive Permission)

Novels Destiny requests the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO permission in strict compliance with the Google Play Store's sensitive permissions guidelines.

How We Handle Your Microphone Audio:

Sole Purpose: Microphone access is utilized exclusively for real-time Speech-to-Text (Voice Dictation), allowing authors to draft novel chapters hands-free and readers to conduct voice-based novel searches.
Zero Audio Storage: Audio input is converted to text instantly using the on-device speech recognizer. Raw audio streams are never stored, never cached on disk, and never transmitted to our databases or third-party servers.
Explicit Consent: The microphone is only activated when you intentionally tap the dictation/microphone button, accompanied by standard Android OS permission prompts.

04

How We Use Collected Data

We process the personal information we collect for the following legitimate business and service purposes:

  • Service Delivery: To display chapter contents, remember your reading font and theme choices, render uploaded PDFs, and synchronize reading positions.
  • Creator Platform Management: To review, verify, and publish author novels and chapter updates under our creator guidelines.
  • Account Security: To authenticate your identity via Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In and protect against fraud and unauthorized account takeover.
  • Notifications: To inform you of newly published episodes from your followed authors, admin approval updates for writers, and critical system announcements.
  • Ad Serving: To serve banner and interstitial ads through Google AdMob, which keeps the vast majority of our novel catalog free for readers worldwide.
  • Performance Optimization: To detect app crashes, measure screen load latencies, and debug technical anomalies.

Advertising Policy (Google AdMob)

Novels Destiny displays advertisements powered by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on your device settings and location (such as European Economic Area or California), AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID) and approximate location to provide personalized or contextual advertisements.

Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or toggle Delete advertising ID.

07

Push Notifications & Messaging

With your permission (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+), we send push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). Notifications inform you about:

  • New chapters published for stories on your reading list.
  • Writer application approval status from the administrative editorial team.
  • Important security and policy announcements.

You can modify notification preferences or turn off push notifications entirely at any time in your device settings under Settings → Apps → Novels Destiny → Notifications.

08

PDF Manuscripts & Cover Images

Novels Destiny provides writers with the capability to attach PDF versions of their chapters and upload custom novel covers.

  • Photo/Storage Access: When you select a cover image or PDF document, the app uses Android's modern photo picker and system file picker. We do not inspect, browse, or read any unrelated files or private images stored on your device.
  • Cloud Storage: Uploaded manuscripts and artwork are securely hosted on Supabase Cloud Storage with scoped read-access rules.
  • Author Ownership: Authors retain complete intellectual property rights to the creative content they upload to Novels Destiny.
09

Data Security & Storage Practices

We deploy multiple layers of administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data:

  • End-to-End Encryption in Transit: All data exchanged between the Novels Destiny mobile client and cloud servers is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security) 1.2 / 1.3 protocol.
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control: Only authorized administrative personnel have permission to review writer applications and moderate reported content.
  • Firestore Security Rules: User documents, reading progress, and private drafts are protected by granular Firestore rules ensuring users can only read and write their own data.
10

Account & Associated Data Deletion

In strict compliance with Google Play's User Data Policy, we believe every user has the absolute right to have their account and associated personal data erased completely.

Two Convenient Ways to Delete Your Account:

Method 1 (In-App Instant Deletion): Open Novels Destiny → Navigate to Profile → Tap Settings → Select Delete Account → Confirm.
Method 2 (Web Request Portal): If you have already uninstalled the app or lost your mobile device, you do not need to reinstall it. Visit our public Account Deletion Request Portal to submit a deletion request online.

Upon account deletion, your authentication record, user profile, reading history, bookmarks, favorites, and unpublished drafts are permanently erased from our production databases within 7 business days.

Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Novels Destiny is designed and intended for general adult and young adult audiences aged 13 years and older (or 16 years and older in the European Economic Area).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has registered an account or provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete that account and associated records from our servers. Parents or guardians who believe their child has registered an account can contact us directly at novelsdestinyadmin@gmail.com for immediate removal.

GDPR & CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic residence, you may have specific statutory privacy rights:

  • European Economic Area & UK (GDPR / UK-GDPR): You have the right to request access to your personal data, rectify inaccuracies, request erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), restrict processing, and receive an export of your data in a portable format.
  • California Residents (CCPA / CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal data. We do not sell your personal data.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please email us with your registered account email at novelsdestinyadmin@gmail.com. We fulfill verified requests within 30 calendar days at no charge.
