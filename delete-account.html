Account & Personal Data Deletion Portal

At Novels Destiny, you have full ownership and autonomy over your data. Whether you want to delete your account directly inside the app or submit an online request because you uninstalled the application, you can initiate permanent deletion below.

Google Play Account Deletion Requirement:
Google requires that apps enabling account creation must allow users to request deletion of their account and associated data through a public web resource, without requiring users to reinstall the application. This page serves as Novels Destiny's certified deletion endpoint.

Method 1: Inside the App

Instant Self-Service Deletion

If you currently have the Novels Destiny app installed on your phone, you can instantly erase your account and stored data in seconds:

  • 1
    Open the Novels Destiny app and make sure you are signed in.
  • 2
    Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  • 3
    Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap Delete Account & Data.
  • 4
    Confirm the final warning modal. Your data and credentials will be purged immediately.

In alignment with Google Play Store policies and international data protection laws (GDPR Art. 17 & CCPA), here is the exact schedule of what happens to your data upon deletion:

Data Category Specific Items Status After Deletion Retention Period & Justification
Authentication Credentials Firebase Auth UID, Email, Password Hash, Google OAuth Access Tokens. Permanently Purged Purged within 24–48 hours. Login access permanently revoked.
User Profile Details Display name, bio, profile avatar photo in cloud storage, role tags. Permanently Purged Completely erased from Cloud Firestore and Supabase avatar storage.
